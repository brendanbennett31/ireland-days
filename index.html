    Why these numbers?

    A day is any day you’re in Ireland, even for an hour. Arrival and departure days both count.

    183 days or more in one year makes you tax resident.

    280 days over this year and last year does too — unless a year has 30 days or fewer, which never counts.

    Your limit is the most days you can stay non-resident. Your target is the limit minus your safety buffer.
